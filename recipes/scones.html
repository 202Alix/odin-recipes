Scones

Description

Quick and tasty English scones!

Ingredients

For 16 samossas

  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 50g of butter
  • 215g f flour
  • 10g of baking powder
  • 5 teaspoons of sugar powder
  • 1 egg
  • 5 tablespoons of milk
  • Some egg yolf or mil to drape on top

Steps

  1. Mix the flour and salt. Using your fingertips, work in the butter. The dough should be very crumbly. Add the sugar, then the egg, and mix with a fork.
  2. Gradually add the milk until you have a fairly firm dough.
  3. Place the dough on a floured board and roll it out to a thickness of about 2.5 cm.
  4. Using a cookie cutter dipped in flour, cut out circles 6 cm in diameter.
  5. Place the scones close together on a baking sheet that has been greased and dusted with flour, in an oven preheated to 230°C (thermostat 7).
  6. Brush them with egg wash or warm milk.
  7. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until they turn golden brown. Remove them from the oven and place them on a wire rack. Cover them immediately with a dish towel to keep them soft and moist.
