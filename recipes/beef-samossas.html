Beef Samossas

Description

Delicious beef samossas with onion, carotts, garlic, ginger and curry spices

We recommend using sweet soy sauce rather than savory.

Ingredients

For 16 samossas

  • 1 and ½ onions
  • 1 and ½ tablespoon of ginger powder
  • vegetable oil
  • olive oil
  • 1 and ½ carrots
  • 3 and ½ tablespoons of curry powder
  • 8 brick sheets
  • 400g of ground beef
  • 1 and ½ garlic cloves
  • 8 tablespoons of soy sauce
  • 1 egg yolk

Steps

  1. In a skillet over high heat, sauté the chopped onion, garlic, and carrot in olive oil.
  2. Add the ground beef and let it brown slightly.
  3. Season with curry powder and ginger, then add the soy sauce. Mix well and cook over low heat for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Divide the filling among the brick pastry sheets (2 samosas per sheet) and fold them (instructions are often provided on the package).
  5. Seal the final fold with egg yolk.
  6. Fry in a shallow pan of oil for 2 minutes on each side.
  7. Once cooked, place the samosas on a plate lined with paper towels to absorb the excess oil. Enjoy!
